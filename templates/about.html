System Architecture & Methodology

About the Flower Classification System

This application integrates dual Deep Learning architectures developed with PyTorch and Hugging Face Transformers to classify images across 102 Oxford Flower species.

How It Works

  1. User selects an architecture (ViT or CNN) and submits a flower photograph.
  2. Flask verifies image integrity and applies the model-specific tensor preprocessing.
  3. Inference executes with torch.inference_mode() and model.eval().
  4. Softmax probabilities compute the top predicted species and ranked candidate scores.
  5. Output labels are decoded through the verified Oxford 102 label mapping.

Vision Transformer (ViT-Base)

  • 🧠 Repository: loretyan/vit-base-oxford-flowers-102
  • 🔍 Architecture: Vision Transformer (12 layers, 12 attention heads)
  • 📦 Patch Size: 16 × 16 patches over 224 × 224 px
  • 🌸 Classes: 102 Oxford Flower categories
  • 🏷️ Decoding: Semantic name-aligned id2label lookup
🧠

Custom CNN (PyTorch Baseline)

  • 🧠 Architecture: 2 Conv blocks (16→32 filters) + 2 Linear layers
  • 💾 Checkpoint: model/flower_classification_model.pth
  • 📦 Preprocessing: 224 × 224 px, ImageNet mean/std normalization
  • 🌸 Classes: 102 Oxford Flower categories
  • ⚡ Speed: Ultra-fast CPU inference (~6.8 ms)
Academic & Botanical Disclaimer: Benchmark accuracy reflects standardized evaluation on the clean Oxford Flowers 102 test set. Real-world images may exhibit variations due to environmental lighting, camera sensor quality, background foliage, or wild botanical hybrids. User uploads are processed temporarily in memory and are never retained permanently.
